Hamburg at a glance How many buildings are in the dataset, how tall are they on average, and how many storeys does a typical Hamburg building have?
 
 ```cypher
MATCH (b:Building)
WITH b, 
     b.ground_z AS ground, 
     coalesce(b.measured_height, 0.0) AS height, 
     (b.ground_z + coalesce(b.measured_height, 0.0)) AS roof_z
WITH b, ground, height, roof_z,
     CASE 
       WHEN ground < 0 THEN 'Below Sea Level (Critical)'
       WHEN ground < 5 THEN 'Low Elevation (High Risk)'
       WHEN ground < 20 THEN 'Mid Elevation (Moderate Risk)'
       ELSE 'High Elevation (Low Risk)'
     END AS elevation_zone
RETURN b.id AS building_id, 
       ground AS ground_elevation, 
       height AS building_height, 
       roof_z AS roof_peak_elevation, 
       elevation_zone
ORDER BY ground ASC
```
